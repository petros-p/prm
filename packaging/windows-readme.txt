PRM - Personal Relationship Manager

TO RUN:

1. Double-click prm.exe.
2. Windows will probably show a blue "Windows protected your PC" warning,
   because this isn't a Microsoft-signed app. Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway". You'll only see this once.
3. A black terminal window opens with PRM running. Type "help" to see
   what you can do, or just follow the prompts - it walks you through
   setup the first time.

Your data is saved next to this file, in a ".data" folder. Nothing
leaves your computer.
