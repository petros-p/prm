PRM - Personal Relationship Manager

TO RUN:

1. Double-click "Run PRM.command".
2. macOS will probably refuse the first time: "cannot be opened because
   it is from an unidentified developer." That's expected for an app
   that isn't distributed through the App Store. Instead of
   double-clicking, right-click (or Control-click) "Run PRM.command"
   and choose "Open", then confirm "Open" again in the dialog. You only
   need to do this once - after that, double-clicking works normally.
3. A terminal window opens with PRM running. Type "help" to see what
   you can do, or just follow the prompts - it walks you through setup
   the first time.

Your data is saved next to this file, in a ".data" folder. Nothing
leaves your computer.
